## Chip specifications

Numbers for Snapdragon 808, Snapdragon 865, and Apple A12 come from public
Qualcomm product briefs, WikiChip, AnandTech, Wikipedia, and ARM documentation.
The Branch Education episode itself notes that it weaves sources together because
vendors hide die photos and floorplans.

Where a slide and a datasheet disagree, the datasheet wins.
Example: the 808 teaching slide labels an Adreno 430 GPU; the shipping 808 uses
**Adreno 418**. Adreno 430 belongs to Snapdragon 810.
